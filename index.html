          林 East Asia, c. 1200–1450 · Ryan Lim · AP World History: Modern, Unit 1 Project

          The map is a 3D relief built from Natural Earth coastlines with painted, exaggerated terrain in the blue-green style of Song landscape painting. The landmarks and ships are miniatures modeled procedurally in Blender, and the chapter paintings were rendered in Blender with Cycles. They are artist’s reconstructions that illustrate the history described here; sizes are exaggerated so they can be seen at map scale.
